# Lead-DBS figure selection

All three figures were downloaded from the official Lead-DBS homepage gallery and visually inspected.

- `fibres-whole-brain.png`: https://www.lead-dbs.org/storage/Fibers_totale_bb-845x684.png — pathways with subcortical structures and MRI sections. Used in the Lead-DBS tools section. The filename is only an internal asset name; this is not a complete whole-brain connectome or a particular downloadable dataset.
- `distal-atlas.png`: https://www.lead-dbs.org/storage/distal_atlas-845x684.png — electrodes with subcortical atlas anatomy. Used on the DBS research page.
- `cover-art.jpg`: https://www.lead-dbs.org/storage/Horn_et_al_coverart_suggestion_small-845x684.jpg — multiple electrode reconstructions, anatomical backdrop and coloured cortical regions. Retained as a candidate, not used as a claim about a specific study.

Source gallery: https://www.lead-dbs.org/ . Scientific colours are retained.
